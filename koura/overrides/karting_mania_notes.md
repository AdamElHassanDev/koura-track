# Karting Mania North, Kousba

## Circuit evidence

- The designer lists Karting Mania North in Kousba as a 2017 circuit design, rental kart and timing system project. The 2017 opening article reports a nominal length of 900 m and maximum width of 8 m. These are opening publicity dimensions. Measure the game geometry against dated imagery. [Circuit designer](https://www.jordan-karting.com/projects.html) · [Lebanon24 opening report and gallery](https://www.lebanon24.com/news/364519)
- The opening overhead photograph shows a full-width checkered start/finish stripe on the short segment between the infield's southern edge and the paddock apron. It also shows alternating red and white kerbs at the two inner U-bends. The promotional close-up shows the checker, a nearby white stripe, red and white portable barriers and the tiled shelter floor. The parked karts in that photo provide no reliable lap heading. [Opening overhead photograph](https://www.lebanon24.com/uploadImages/GalleryImages/PGB-364519-6367055813374958381280x960.jpg) · [Paddock close-up](https://makhsoom.com/lb/storage/160000/134756/68e93a05b03c87e26930350e732c617d.jpg)
- In the 2017 car-event footage, at 01:00 and 01:35–01:45 the infield edges have red and white kerbs and the outer fence has tyre stacks and banners. At 01:20 the course has red and white portable barrier blocks, dark tyre stacks, green mesh fence, floodlight poles and a red lattice support. These are useful references for asset appearance. The footage establishes the car event's setup for that day. [Gilbert Sayde, FRTC Super Speed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DTAbxZPpLi8)
- The 2024 Pixel Zone footage shows the red-roof building, faded barrier blocks, broad patched asphalt and dark skid marks at a motorcycle session held at the Kousba venue. The ATCL bulletin separately confirms a drift event there on 11 September 2022. The motorcycle and drift events do not establish the current rental layout. [Pixel Zone footage](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C--nS2bMOAw/) · [Kousba venue section](https://www.lebanontraveler.com/en/magazine/where-to-go-karting-in-lebanon/) · [2022 ATCL bulletin](https://atclmotorsports.com/uploads/documents/events/631848db2d3ac_2022%20Drift%203%20-%20Bulletin%204.pdf)
- The branded rental-kart photos show molded red and white divider blocks, alternating painted tyre stacks, a red-and-white lattice mast, slender white standards and a dark rectangular signboard outside a bend. The 2019 390 Cup night photograph confirms white floodlights operating over the circuit, with the red-and-white kerb and tyre treatment visible beside the track. [Rental karts in motion](https://makhsoom.com/lb/storage/160000/133217/fd04dcdb069e40878f2b33b2f048531f.jpg) · [Kart lineup photograph](https://guide.moovtoo.com/storage/galleries/Venue/karting-mania-north-19109/Karting%20Mania%20North/karting-mania-north-19109-2023-05-18-6466496e0712b.jpeg) · [2019 390 Cup night photo](https://www.snobarabia.net/page.php?id=6650)
- An undated visitor photograph by Tamer Qusous shows a kart on the paddock-side straight with the nose pointing image right and the driver behind it. The paddock railing is in the foreground and the infield with its lattice light mast is beyond the checker. This is consistent with eastbound travel over the checker and an anticlockwise course, viewed from the south side. This establishes a single observed heading. Other gallery photos show the checker from the restaurant terrace, white floodlight clusters on red lattice masts, red cross-braced railings, a white-background venue banner and a dark signboard beside the stepped viewing area. ## Visible segments in World Imagery

The current aerial clearly shows a gap between the paddock divider runs. The opening photograph shows a different curved divider and the adjoining paddock apron. Neither view resolves the current pit flow, the pit stall coordinates or the legal merge path. Ground-level confirmation is needed before assigning those positions and headings.

One probable floodlight mast foot is visible at `(486,1127)`, approximately `34.326198, 35.852439`, in the southwest part of the infield north of the checker. The aerial shows a small light foot connected to a narrow dark stem with a bright upper feature. The terrace photos show a tapering lattice mast with a tyre collar and closely spaced directional lights in that same infield. The placement is an imagery estimate within a few pixels. An 8 m above-ground model shaft is an initial visual estimate from the pictured trackside scale. A ground survey is needed for height and fixture specifications. ## Timing evidence still needed

- The old photograph establishes a checkered line on the paddock-side straight. A rough transfer from the oblique opening photograph places it in the vicinity of reference pixel `(530,1170)`, about `34.32610, 35.85256`. The rough fit had control-point residuals of several metres. Current aerials do not resolve its paint or show a full race grid. Current endpoints and any painted starting bays require a close overhead pass or geolocated ground photographs.
- An uninterrupted rental kart lap or a venue plan with arrows is needed to confirm the present operating direction. The attempted 2017 venue opening video is unavailable and the LBCI player supplies an unavailable-video slate. [Opening video link](https://www.facebook.com/KartingManiaLebanon/videos/355433931555065/) · [LBCI opening report](https://www.lbcgroup.tv/news/334366)
- A current view of karts leaving and returning to the paddock would resolve the opening used at each end and the pit merge and exit in the driving direction.
